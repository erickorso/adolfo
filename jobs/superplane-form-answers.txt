## What's the most impactful thing you've built and shipped in the last two years?

Pluvia at Krunchbox — a B2B retail analytics product where buyers explore sales and inventory across stores, categories, and time. Early versions froze on filter changes with 100k+ rows, so users fled to Excel.

I owned the frontend architecture end to end: moved the grid to ag-Grid server-side row model, defined a stable filter/sort/pagination contract with backend, added short-lived cache keys for common queries, and tightened UX (debounced filters, fixed KPI columns). Typical filter ops dropped under ~300ms and retailers could explore in-product again.

In parallel I shipped a Next.js application shell for an AI assistant with streaming state that survives navigation across 30+ routes — so AI UX didn't remount and lose in-flight chats. That mix of product taste, performance, and AI-in-the-loop shipping is how I work day to day (also on Adolfo: https://github.com/erickorso/adolfo · https://adolfo-nine.vercel.app).
